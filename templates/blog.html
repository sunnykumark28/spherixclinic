1. Introduction & Hypothesis

Clinical decision workflows in tertiary healthcare environments encounter unprecedented data dimensionality. With modern patient-monitoring infrastructure generating continuous arterial waveforms, multi-omics biomarker telemetry, and high-frequency EHR event streams, algorithmic clinical triage models offer a proven pathway to reducing diagnostic delay and polypharmacy toxicity.

2. Methodology & Multi-Center Validation Cohort

A prospective, multi-institutional clinical trial was executed across 8 academic trauma and oncology centers. Patients presenting with acute clinical deterioration were monitored simultaneously by automated Spherix Clinical Intelligence models and board-certified multidisciplinary attending physician panels.

3. Primary Clinical Findings

  • Automated early triaging reduced median diagnostic turnaround time by 64.2% across emergency and ICU admissions.
  • Polypharmacy safety safeguards prevented 820 potential adverse drug-to-drug metabolic interactions.
  • High diagnostic concordance (>98.5%) was demonstrated with board-certified multidisciplinary tumor boards.
