Restorative Oncology Care

Cancer Recovery & Physical Rehabilitation

Specialized rehabilitation programs designed to restore physical mobility, alleviate lymphedema, overcome cancer-related fatigue, and optimize long-term quality of life.

Restoration Modules

Targeted Oncology Rehabilitation Pathways

Lymphedema Management

Complete Decongestive Therapy (CDT) combining manual lymphatic drainage (MLD), multi-layer compression bandaging, and custom-fitted garments to manage fluid retention after lymph node dissection.

Target: Breast & Pelvic Resections

Post-Op Physiotherapy

Early mobilization, scar tissue massage, shoulder range-of-motion therapy for axillary web syndrome, and chest wall expansion exercises to prevent musculoskeletal stiffness.

Target: Thoracic & Breast Surgery

Cancer-Related Fatigue

Evidence-based moderate aerobic conditioning (15-30 min gentle walking), sleep architecture hygiene, pacing techniques, and cognitive-behavioral energy conservation.

Target: Chemo & Radiation Patients

Pelvic Floor & Continence

Biofeedback-assisted pelvic floor muscle training (Kegel exercises) to restore urinary continence and bowel regularity following radical prostatectomy or colorectal resection.

Target: Prostate, Colorectal & GYN
Long-Term Health Protection

Survivorship Surveillance & Organ Health

Cardio-Oncology Monitoring

Echocardiograms and strain imaging every 6-12 months for patients who received anthracyclines (Doxorubicin) or anti-HER2 therapies (Trastuzumab) to ensure optimal left ventricular ejection fraction.

Bone Density (DEXA) Health

Annual dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry scans during aromatase inhibitor (Letrozole) or androgen deprivation therapy (Leuprolide) to prevent osteopenia and pathological fractures.

Neurocognitive Recovery

Structured cognitive retraining, memory adaptation strategies, and executive function exercises to resolve treatment-associated cognitive changes ("chemo-brain").
