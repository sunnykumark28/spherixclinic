System Manual: Core Identity v4

1. Authentication

The authentication layer uses a dual-token JWT system. Upon login, the client receives an Access Token (15m expiry) and a Refresh Token (7d expiry). All medical endpoints require `Bearer` auth.

2. Role Propagation

Roles are stored as bitmasks in the database. `Patient=1`, `Doctor=2`, `Admin=4`. This allows for multi-role users (e.g., a Doctor who is also a Patient) without data duplication.

3. Media Handling

Prescriptions and ID documents are stored in encrypted S3 buckets. Presigned URLs are generated on-the-fly with a 60-second TTL to prevent unauthorized sharing.

4. Localization

The system automatically detects user locale and timezone. All timestamps in the database are stored in UTC and converted at the presentation layer using Luxon.js.

Telehealth Support FAQ

What happens if my internet fails?

The session context is preserved in Redis. Simply refresh the page within 5 minutes to automatically reconnect to the same session with all previous chat history intact.

Is the video recorded?

No. For maximum privacy, we utilize P2P streams that never touch our persistent storage. Only the clinical notes, chat transcripts, and prescriptions are archived in our encrypted database.

Can I join via mobile?

Yes, our interface is built with a Mobile-First approach. The WebRTC implementation is compatible with Safari (iOS) and Chrome (Android) without needing external apps.

How do I receive my prescription?

Once the doctor ends the session and uploads the file, a notification appears on your dashboard. You can download it as a signed PDF or send it directly to an E-Pharmacy.

AI Diagnostic Guidelines

Is it a replacement for a doctor?

No. Our AI is designed for Triage and Information only. It prepares the patient for the doctor by gathering data and suggesting possibilities, but a human physician always makes the final diagnosis.

How is my data used?

Your symptom data is anonymized and used to help track regional outbreaks. Personal identifiers are stripped before the data ever reaches the training models.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

The system includes a Conservative Bias, meaning it is programmed to overestimate severity to ensure patients are directed to professional help whenever any doubt exists.

E-Pharmacy Operator Guidelines

1. Order Processing: Orders are automatically routed to the nearest partner pharmacy based on the user's geolocation. The system uses a Haversine formula to calculate the optimal fulfillment node.

2. Substitution Logic: If a specific brand is out of stock, the system suggests generic equivalents based on active chemical ingredients (APIs), but only after a clinical cross-check.

3. Quality Control: Sellers must upload high-resolution photos of the medication packaging and expiry date before dispatch. These are saved to the order record for user verification.

4. Return Policy: Due to health safety regulations, medications are non-returnable once the tamper-evident seal is broken. Damaged goods are eligible for instant credits.
