Precision Diagnostic Oncology

Advanced Diagnostic Testing & TNM Staging

Accurate oncological diagnosis requires multimodality precision: combining high-resolution cross-sectional imaging, guided tissue histopathology, comprehensive genomic profiling, and AJCC TNM anatomical staging.

The Diagnostic Arsenal

Gold-Standard Diagnostic Pillars

Histopathologic Biopsy

Core needle, stereotactic, and excisional biopsies provide intact tumor architecture for microscopic grading and definitive histologic confirmation.

Modality: Ultrasound/CT-guided core

NGS Genomic Profiling

Comprehensive next-generation sequencing across 500+ oncogenes identifying actionable mutations (EGFR, BRAF, KRAS, HER2) for targeted therapy.

Modality: NextSeq NGS & Liquid ctDNA

Whole-Body PET-CT

18F-FDG and 68Ga-PSMA molecular PET scans detect hypermetabolic tumor glucose uptake and nodal micrometastasis throughout the entire body.

Modality: Biograph Horizon PET-CT

3T Functional MRI

Multiparametric diffusion-weighted MRI delivers sub-millimeter soft tissue resolution essential for prostate, brain, rectal, and breast staging.

Modality: 3 Tesla Magnetom MRI
