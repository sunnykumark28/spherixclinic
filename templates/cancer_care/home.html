Multidisciplinary Consensus

Every Patient Case is Reviewed by Our Full Tumor Board

No single oncologist makes critical cancer decisions in isolation. Spherix operates weekly Multidisciplinary Tumor Boards (MDT) uniting surgical oncologists, medical oncologists, radiation physicists, molecular pathologists, radiologists, and oncology nurse navigators.

  • Evidence-based staging aligned with NCCN and ESMO algorithms
  • Precision matching to molecular and genomic clinical trials
  • Organ-preservation strategies and minimized surgical morbidity
