What to do in an emergency?

  • 1 Stay calm and call for help immediately.
  • 2 Provide your location clearly to the operator.
  • 3 Do not move the patient unless there is immediate danger (e.g., fire).
  • 4 If trained, administer First Aid or CPR as necessary.
