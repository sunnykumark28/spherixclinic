01 • Definition, Mission & Purpose

Re-Engineering Healthcare as an Instant, Intelligent Event System

In Short: What is Spherix Clinic?

Spherix Clinic is a unified, intelligent healthcare operating system designed to bridge the critical gap between patients, licensed doctors, accredited hospitals, pharmacies, diagnostic labs, and emergency dispatch. It combines real-time infrastructure (like hospital ICU bed tracking, waiting room TV kiosks, and 10km GPS emergency triage) with multi-modal Artificial Intelligence (including nutrition planning, symptom differential diagnosis, radiological image review, and OpenFDA drug-interaction safety).

Traditional healthcare is deeply fragmented. When a patient falls ill, their journey is disjointed: preliminary searching leads to misleading advice, booking a specialist involves long wait times, hospital bed availability is opaque during emergencies, pharmacy prescriptions may have undetected adverse drug-drug interactions, and medical records remain trapped in physical paper files.

Spherix Clinic resolves this fragmentation entirely. Every component of medical care is synchronized across a zero-latency health cloud. A patient can evaluate symptoms with clinical-grade AI, consult with a board-certified physician over encrypted WebRTC video, receive a digital prescription screened against 11,825+ OpenFDA medicines, order doorstep cold-chain medications with live GPS tracking, and store their longitudinal health records securely under Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and HIPAA-aligned encryption standards.

02 • Operational Workflow & System Elements

How Spherix Clinic Works: Complete End-to-End Journey

Spherix Clinic operates on a real-time event pipeline where each step seamlessly feeds into the next, eliminating manual delays and medical errors.

01

Intelligent Triaging & Symptom Stratification

The patient describes symptoms or uploads lab reports. The system evaluates against 5,125+ MedQuAD condition profiles, checks for emergency red flags (e.g. cardiac distress, stroke indicators), and assigns a clinical urgency score.

02

Board-Certified Doctor Consultation & E-Prescription

The patient connects with a specialist via encrypted 4K WebRTC video or in-clinic appointment. The physician writes an e-prescription through the smart RX builder, where every medication is screened in real-time against OpenFDA databases for Drug-Drug (DDI) and Food-Drug interactions.

03

Cold-Chain Pharmacy Dispatch & Live GPS Tracking

Prescriptions flow directly to accredited partner pharmacies. Medications are packed under temperature-monitored cold-chain standards (vital for insulin and biologics) and dispatched with real-time courier GPS tracking to the patient's doorstep.

04

Longitudinal Health Locker & AI Nutrition Maintenance

All consultation notes, lab trends, and prescriptions are encrypted in the patient's sovereign health locker. The Spherix Health Intelligence Nutrition & Fitness engine continuously analyzes metabolic parameters to generate ongoing preventive dietary and exercise blueprints.

04 • Core Questions, Answers & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions About Spherix Clinic

Clear, authoritative answers to common inquiries regarding clinical accuracy, patient safety, hospital registrations, emergency dispatches, and pricing.

Q1

What is Spherix Clinic and what problems does it solve?

Spherix Clinic is an integrated digital health intelligence platform engineered to unify clinical consultations, emergency telemetry, hospital operations, online pharmacy delivery, and biomedical AI models into a single zero-latency ecosystem.

It solves the severe fragmentation in traditional healthcare where diagnostic results, pharmacy prescriptions, emergency ambulances, and hospital bed availability operate in isolated silos, causing treatment delays and medical errors.

Q2

Is Spherix Clinic AI a replacement for human doctors?

No. Spherix Clinic AI operates under the clinical paradigm of Augmented Intelligence (AI-as-a-Partner). It acts as a decision support and preliminary triage tool to help patients understand symptoms and assist clinicians with differential reasoning and contraindication screening.

All formal diagnoses, clinical prescriptions, and treatment regimens must be finalized and signed by a licensed, board-certified physician.

Q3

How does the AI ensure clinical accuracy and prevent hallucinations?

Spherix Clinic anchors its neural models against validated clinical knowledge repositories:

  • MedQuAD & PubMed: Over 5,125 verified disease profiles with Bayesian probability filtering.
  • OpenFDA Drug Database: 11,825+ pharmaceutical monographs for pharmacological verification.
  • Strict Guardrails: Emergency red-flag detection (chest pain, stroke symptoms) immediately interrupts processing to trigger emergency SOS routing.
Q4

How does the Spherix Health Intelligence Nutrition & Fitness Planner work?

The Nutrition & Fitness Planner utilizes standard metabolic equations:

  • BMR: Mifflin-St Jeor equation customized for age, gender, height, and weight.
  • TDEE: Adjusted for physical activity multipliers (1.2 to 1.9).
  • Body Fat: Deurenberg formula incorporating BMI, age, and sex.
  • Diet Synthesis: Generates complete 7-day macronutrient meal plans, targeted resistance workouts, and grocery lists with continuous 24/7 AI nutritionist coaching.
Q5

How is patient data and Protected Health Information (PHI) secured?

Spherix Clinic implements bank-grade cryptographic standards:

  • 2048-Bit RSA & TLS 1.3: Encrypts all data in transit across WebRTC and REST endpoints.
  • AES-256 GCM: Encrypts uploaded lab reports and electronic medical records at rest.
  • HIPAA & ABDM: Strict role-based access control preventing unauthorized data leakage.
Q6

How does the Live Hospital Bed Tracker and OPD Queue TV Kiosk work?

Registered hospitals update their bed capacity (ICU, ventilator, oxygen, general ward) in real-time through their administrative dashboard. Patients and emergency responders can see current vacancies on the live map.

The OPD Live Queue TV Kiosk provides hospital waiting rooms with a dedicated full-screen display mode that calls patient tokens automatically, reducing physical congestion and patient anxiety.
