Supportive Oncology Care

Treatment Companion & Side-Effect Guide

Evidence-based protocols to manage chemotherapy, radiation, and immunotherapy side effects with maximum comfort.

Nausea & Appetite Loss

Take prescribed 5-HT3 / NK-1 receptor antiemetics 30 min before meals. Opt for small, cool, low-odor meals (crackers, ginger tea, cold fruit smoothies).

💡 Tip: Avoid large fatty meals and sip cold electrolyte water continuously.

Infection & Neutropenia

When white blood cells drop, practice rigorous hand hygiene. Cook all meats and vegetables thoroughly. Avoid crowded indoor spaces and raw shellfish.

⚠️ Red Flag: If temperature exceeds 100.4°F (38.0°C), go to the ER immediately.

Peripheral Neuropathy

Tingling or numbness in fingers and toes from taxane or platinum drugs. Use warm gloves, avoid touching frozen items directly, and wear supportive shoes.

💡 Tip: Cryotherapy (cooling mittens/socks) during chemo infusion reduces incidence.

Mouth Sores (Mucositis)

Rinse 4-6 times daily with a gentle baking soda and salt solution (1 tsp each per 1 quart water). Avoid alcohol-based mouthwashes and spicy/acidic foods.

💡 Tip: Soft lukewarm foods (yogurt, oatmeal, pureed soup) minimize irritation.

Cancer-Related Fatigue

Pacing is key. Engage in low-intensity daily walks (15-20 min), prioritize nighttime sleep, and schedule important tasks during morning peak energy hours.

💡 Tip: Light regular exercise is clinically proven to reduce chemo fatigue up to 35%.

Radiation Dermatitis

Clean treated skin gently with lukewarm water and fragrance-free soap. Apply pure aloe vera gel or prescribed calendula cream (do not apply 2 hrs before radiation).

💡 Tip: Wear loose-fitting, 100% breathable cotton clothing over treatment areas.
Supportive Care Policy: Side-effect strategies must be coordinated with your oncology care team. Never discontinue prescribed antiemetics without physician consultation.
