Patient & Family Support Services

Cancer impacts the entire family unit. Spherix Clinic provides dedicated psycho-oncology counseling, caregiver burnout support, financial navigation, and government scheme facilitation.

Psycho-Oncology Counseling

Compassionate psychological support for newly diagnosed and active patients

Facing a cancer diagnosis can trigger intense shock, anticipatory anxiety, and emotional vulnerability. Our board-certified psycho-oncologists provide one-on-one therapy to help patients process emotional distress, build adaptive coping mechanisms, and manage treatment fears.

Key Areas of Focus:
  • Overcoming fear of treatment procedures and needle phobia
  • Managing body image shifts following mastectomies or ostomy creation
  • Addressing existential concerns and restoring personal agency

Caregiver Resilience & Support

Nurturing those who provide primary care to their loved ones

Caregivers often neglect their own emotional and physical well-being while managing complex medication schedules, hospital appointments, and emotional demands. We provide specialized respite resources and practical checklists.

Practical Caregiver Rules:
  • Schedule mandatory 30-minute daily personal respite breaks
  • Delegate specific tasks (grocery shopping, rides) to extended family
  • Attend monthly caregiver peer group circles for emotional decompression

Financial Navigation

Government Financial Schemes & Subsidies

Spherix Clinic's social service desk assists eligible families in accessing critical financial aid schemes.

PM-JAY Scheme

Ayushman Bharat

Cashless coverage up to ₹5,00,000 per family annually for secondary and tertiary oncology surgical procedures, chemotherapy, and radiotherapy.

Central Fund

Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi

One-time financial assistance up to ₹15,00,000 for poor patients living below the poverty line suffering from life-threatening cancer conditions.

Specialized Grant

Health Minister's Fund

HMCPF provides financial grants up to ₹5,00,000 directly to Regional Cancer Centres for low-income patients receiving active treatment.

Travel Concession

Railway Concessions

Up to 75% to 100% concession on Indian Railways train tickets for cancer patients and their designated travel escorts for medical treatment.
