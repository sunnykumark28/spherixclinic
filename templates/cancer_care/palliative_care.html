Compassionate Supportive Medicine

Palliative & Supportive Oncology Care

Palliative care is specialized medical care focused on relief from the symptoms, pain, and physical/emotional stress of cancer. It is integrated alongside curative treatment at any stage of illness.

Critical Clinical Clarification

Palliative Care Is NOT Giving Up — It Is Concurrent Supportive Excellence

A common misconception is that palliative care is reserved exclusively for the final days of life. Landmark clinical studies (Temel et al., NEJM) prove that introducing palliative care early alongside standard chemotherapy and radiation significantly improves patient survival, functional independence, and overall quality of life.

Evidence-Based Analgesia

The WHO Three-Step Cancer Pain Ladder

Systematic pharmacotherapy calibrated to eliminate pain while maintaining lucidity and alertness.

Step 1 • Mild Pain

Non-Opioid Analgesics

Acetaminophen (Paracetamol) and nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) with gastric mucosal protection. Often combined with adjuvant analgesics for nerve pain.

Numeric Pain Score: 1 to 3
Step 2 • Moderate Pain

Mild Opioids for Pain

Weak opioid agonists such as Tramadol, Codeine, or low-dose oral formulations, paired with bowel regimens to prevent constipation.

Numeric Pain Score: 4 to 6
Step 3 • Severe Pain

Potent Opioids & Nerve Blocks

Individualized round-the-clock Morphine, Fentanyl transdermal patches, or Oxycodone with rescue doses for breakthrough pain; CT-guided celiac plexus neurolysis.

Numeric Pain Score: 7 to 10

Breathlessness & Dyspnea Relief

Alleviating respiratory distress and chest tightness

Low-dose oral morphine reduces the sensation of air hunger without suppressing respiratory drive. Portable hand-held facial fans, positioning, and oxygen therapy provide rapid comfort.

Home Hospice & Nurse Visits

Dignified, peaceful care in the familiar comfort of home

Our home palliative nursing team visits patients at home for catheter care, subcutaneous medication administration, wound dressings, and emotional support for the family.
