Preparation & Post-Care

Clinical advice for safe and comfortable donation

Before Donation:
  • Drink at least 500ml water 1-2 hours prior.
  • Eat a light, healthy meal within 3 hours. Avoid fatty foods.
  • Ensure 6-8 hours of sound sleep prior to donation.
After Donation:
  • Rest for 10-15 minutes in the clinic recovery lounge.
  • Keep bandage clean and dry for 4-5 hours.
  • Avoid heavy lifting or intense workouts for 24 hours.
