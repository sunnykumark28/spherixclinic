Clinical Oncology Dietetics

Oncology Nutrition & Integrative Wellness

Specialized dietary protocols engineered to maintain lean muscle mass, counteract cancer-induced cachexia, prevent neutropenic foodborne infections, and manage treatment toxicities.

Critical Safety Standard

The Neutropenic Diet Protocol

When chemotherapy reduces the Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC < 1,000 cells/µL), the immune system cannot fight bacterial or fungal pathogens normally found on uncooked food. Follow these strict rules during low counts:

Strictly Thoroughly Cooked Food All meats, poultry, seafood, and eggs must be cooked to internal temperatures ≥165°F (74°C). Zero raw sushi, rare meats, or runny soft-boiled egg yolks.
Avoid Raw Salads & Unpeeled Fruits Raw leafy greens (lettuce, spinach salads) can harbor Listeria and E. coli. Consume only cooked vegetables and thick-skinned fruits that are peeled (bananas, oranges).
Pasteurized Dairy & Safe Water Only consume pasteurized milk, yogurts, and cheeses. Avoid soft unpasteurized cheeses (brie, blue cheese) and unboiled tap water outside approved hospital filters.
Symptom Management

Dietary Solutions for Treatment Side Effects

Nausea & Queasiness
  • • Eat small, frequent meals (6-8 snacks/day) rather than large heavy portions.
  • • Consume dry, bland foods: crackers, toast, dry oatmeal.
  • • Sip cooled ginger tea or clear broths; avoid overly greasy or pungent odors.
Mouth Sores & Mucositis
  • • Choose soft, cool foods: room-temperature purees, smoothies, puddings.
  • • Strictly avoid acidic items: tomatoes, citrus fruits, spicy curries, and rough crackers.
  • • Rinse mouth with non-alcoholic salt and baking soda solution after every meal.
Metallic Taste (Dysgeusia)
  • • Use bamboo, wooden, or plastic cutlery instead of metallic silverware.
  • • Marinate proteins in mild tart marinades, mint, or mild herbal rubs.
  • • Chew sugar-free tart lemon or cinnamon drops to cleanse taste receptors.
Weight Loss & Muscle Wasting
  • • Target 1.2 to 1.5 grams of high-biological-value protein per kilogram daily.
  • • Enrich dishes with healthy calorie boosters: nut butters, olive oil, avocado.
  • • Incorporate specialized medical nutrition whey or plant protein supplements.
Dry Mouth (Xerostomia)
  • • Add extra gravies, broths, and mild sauces to soften swallowed textures.
  • • Carry a water bottle and take frequent sips throughout the day.
  • • Use artificial saliva sprays and overnight bedside humidifiers.
Diarrhea & Dehydration
  • • Follow the BRAT diet (Bananas, Rice, Applesauce, Toast) during loose stools.
  • • Replenish electrolytes using oral rehydration solutions (ORS) and coconut water.
  • • Temporarily restrict insoluble wheat bran, raw beans, and caffeine.
Integrative Oncology

Mind-Body Practices for Stress & Resilience

Oncology Restorative Yoga

Gentle, non-strenuous seated and supine somatic flows that downregulate sympathetic nervous arousal, calm cortisol levels, and improve joint flexibility without joint compression.

Guided Breathwork & Pranayama

Diaphragmatic pacing (4-7-8 breathing) that stimulates the parasympathetic vagus nerve, reducing procedural anxiety prior to CT scans or radiotherapy sessions.

Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction

Clinically validated MBSR protocols shown in ASCO clinical trials to reduce insomnia, alleviate depressive symptoms, and enhance overall emotional well-being.
