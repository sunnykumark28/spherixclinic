Public Oncology Health Literacy

Cancer Awareness, Early Detection & Prevention

Cancer is often curable when detected at an early, localized stage. Empowering yourself and your family with knowledge of clinical warning signs is the single most effective step toward life-saving intervention.

Clinical Mnemonic

The 7 CAUTION Warning Signs

Developed by the American Cancer Society and adopted globally by clinical oncologists.

Symptoms Checker
C

Change in Bowel or Bladder Habits

Alternating diarrhea and constipation lasting >3 weeks, pencil-thin stools, sudden urinary hesitancy, or nocturnal frequency without prior cause.

Suspected: Colorectal, Bladder, Prostate
A

A Sore That Does Not Heal

Non-healing skin ulcers, bleeding patches, or persistent oral mouth sores and tongue ulcers lasting longer than 14-21 days despite topical treatment.

Suspected: Skin (Melanoma/BCC), Oral Cavity
U

Unusual Bleeding or Discharge

Post-menopausal vaginal bleeding, bloody nipple discharge, hematuria (blood in urine), hemoptysis (coughing up blood), or rectal maroon melena.

Suspected: Cervical, Uterine, Lung, Renal
T

Thickening or Lump in Body

Firm, painless palpable lumps in the breast, axilla, neck, testicle, or groin that feel anchored or progressively enlarge over weeks.

Suspected: Breast, Lymphoma, Sarcoma
I

Indigestion or Dysphagia

Difficulty swallowing solids or liquids, sensation of food sticking in retrosternal esophagus, or unrelenting upper stomach dyspepsia with early satiety.

Suspected: Esophageal, Gastric, Pancreatic
O

Obvious Change in Wart or Mole

Moles exhibiting asymmetry, irregular notched borders, varied colors (brown, black, blue, pink), diameter >6mm, or surface bleeding (ABCDE rule).

Suspected: Cutaneous Melanoma
N

Nagging Cough or Hoarseness

Persistent hacking cough unresponsive to standard antibiotics lasting >3 weeks, unprovoked hoarseness, or recurrent chest infections.

Suspected: Lung, Larynx, Thyroid

Systemic B-Symptoms

Unexplained weight loss >10% within 6 months, drenching night sweats requiring sheet changes, and intermittent high fevers.

Suspected: Lymphoma, Leukemia, Advanced Stage

Dispelling Misconceptions

Oncology Myths vs. Evidence-Based Facts

MYTH: "A biopsy or needle puncture will make cancer spread rapidly."
FACT: Modern core needle biopsies are performed using coaxial needle guides that seal the biopsy tract and prevent tumor cell seeding. A biopsy is mandatory to obtain molecular biomarkers (HER2, EGFR, PD-L1) without which targeted curative therapy cannot be administered.
MYTH: "Cancer is contagious and can be caught by living with a patient."
FACT: Cancer is not an infectious disease and cannot be transmitted through hugging, sharing utensils, or caring for a patient. Certain viruses (HPV, Hepatitis B) increase risk, but the malignant cells themselves cannot spread between people.
MYTH: "Cutting out all sugar from diet will starve and cure cancer."
FACT: All human cells, including healthy immune T-cells, require glucose for survival. Extreme glucose deprivation starves the immune system and induces severe malnutrition without stopping tumor growth. A balanced Mediterranean diet is medically advised.
MYTH: "Cancer is always a death sentence."
FACT: Millions of cancer survivors live long, healthy lives. With modern immunotherapy, targeted TKIs, and robotic surgeries, many cancers are either completely cured or managed as chronic controllable conditions with normal lifespans.
