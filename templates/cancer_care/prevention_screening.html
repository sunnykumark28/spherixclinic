Proactive Clinical Oncology

Cancer Prevention & Screening Guidelines

Up to 50% of all cancers are preventable through lifestyle risk mitigation and timely screening that identifies precancerous lesions before malignant transformation.

Primary Prevention

5 Core Pillars of Cancer Risk Reduction

Zero Tobacco Use

Eliminate all cigarette, bidi, gutkha, and smokeless tobacco products. Tobacco accounts for ~85% of lung cancers and 40% of overall malignancies.

Mediterranean Diet

High dietary fiber (≥30g/day), cruciferous greens, legumes, and omega-3s. Eliminate ultra-processed meats, cured bacon, and excessive alcohol.

Weight & Exercise

Maintain normal BMI (18.5 - 24.9) and achieve ≥150 minutes of moderate aerobic exercise weekly. Adipose inflammation drives 13 common cancers.

UV Solar Shielding

Apply broad-spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen, wear protective clothing, and avoid midday solar irradiance to prevent cutaneous melanoma.

Viral Vaccination

Administer 9-valent HPV vaccine for adolescents (cervical/oral cancer prevention) and infant Hepatitis B vaccine (liver cancer prevention).

Cancer Genetics Clinic

Hereditary Cancer Syndromes & Multi-Gene Panels

Approximately 10% of cancers are hereditary, caused by germline mutations passed across generations. Identifying these mutations enables proactive risk-reducing interventions (such as enhanced MRI surveillance, chemoprevention, or prophylactic surgery).

BRCA1 / BRCA2 Elevated Breast, Ovarian, Prostate & Pancreatic risk.
Lynch Syndrome MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2 mutations driving early Colorectal & Endometrial cancer.

Who Should Seek Genetic Counseling?

  • First-degree relative diagnosed with cancer under age 50
  • Multiple relatives with breast, ovarian, colon, or pancreatic cancers
  • Personal history of rare cancers (e.g., male breast cancer)
