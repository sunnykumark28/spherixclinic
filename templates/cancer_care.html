Supportive Care Guidelines

Chemotherapy & Radiation Care Companion

Practical, evidence-based recommendations to navigate treatment side effects with maximum comfort and safety.

Nausea & Appetite Loss

Take prescribed 5-HT3 / NK-1 receptor antiemetics 30 min before meals. Choose small, cool, low-odor meals like crackers, ginger tea, or fruit smoothies.

Tip: Avoid hot oily meals and sip cold electrolyte fluids steadily throughout the day.

Infection & Neutropenia

When absolute neutrophil counts (ANC) drop, practice rigorous hand hygiene. Ensure all foods are thoroughly cooked and avoid crowded indoor spaces.

Warning: Any fever ≥100.4°F (38.0°C) requires emergency evaluation immediately.

Peripheral Neuropathy

Numbness or tingling in fingers and toes from platinum/taxane drugs. Wear warm gloves, avoid touching ice directly, and choose wide-toed shoes.

Tip: Cryotherapy (cooling mittens during infusion) significantly reduces peripheral nerve damage.

Oral Mucositis & Sores

Rinse 4-6 times daily with a mild salt & baking soda solution. Avoid alcohol-based mouthwashes and spicy or acidic foods.

Tip: Soft, cool meals like Greek yogurt and pureed vegetable soups soothe delicate oral mucosa.

Cancer-Related Fatigue

Engage in gentle daily walks (15-20 minutes). Prioritize consistent sleep cycles and schedule demanding tasks during your peak morning energy window.

Tip: Light physical activity is clinically shown to reduce cancer fatigue by up to 35%.

Radiation Dermatitis

Wash irradiated skin gently with lukewarm water and mild fragrance-free soap. Apply pure aloe vera or recommended calendula lotion.

Tip: Wear loose 100% breathable cotton clothes and never apply lotion within 2 hours prior to radiation.
